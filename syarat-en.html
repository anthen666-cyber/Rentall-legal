4. Rental flow

The renter sends a request → the owner approves and sends the meeting point and payment info → the renter pays the owner directly → the owner marks the payment as received → physical handover → return → the owner confirms completion. Handover is always done in person, not by shipping. We recommend meeting in a busy public place.

  • A request that is approved but not paid within 24 hours expires automatically.
  • If the owner does not respond to the return within 24 hours, the rental is considered complete automatically.
  • The damage deposit is an agreement between the owner and the renter that is recorded in the app. It is settled directly between the two of them.
  • Transactions worth Rp500,000 and above (rental price plus deposit) require the renter to complete Level 2 Verification (ID card and selfie).

5. The Needs feature

  • Users can write down an item or service they need (maximum 240 characters) with a budget. Up to 5 verified offer-makers can make offers; the requester can decline offers so slots open up.
  • Phone numbers become visible to both parties only after an offer is chosen.
  • The provider marks the job as done, the requester confirms (automatically confirmed if there is no response within 24 hours), and then both can review each other.

6. Problem reports and automatic sanctions

  • A damage report by the owner must be filed no later than 24 hours after the item is returned, with evidence. The renter has 48 hours to admit or dispute it (a dispute must include a reason). The loss estimate is only a record.
  • An item not received report by the renter: the owner has 24 hours to confirm that the item has been handed over. If there is no response, the booking is cancelled automatically.
  • Violation points (valid for 90 days): admitting damage, not responding to a damage report within 48 hours, or an owner not responding to an item not received report within 24 hours are each worth 1 point. A disputed report does not add points but is recorded as a dispute on both parties' profiles.
  • Automatic penalties: 2 points results in a warning; 3 points restricts the account for 30 days (it cannot rent, and its items are hidden from search); the next restriction lasts 90 days.
