RENTALL Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026 · Bahasa Indonesia

RENTALL is a marketplace app that connects owners of items/services with renters in Indonesia. This policy explains what data we collect, what we use it for, who it is shared with, and your rights over that data.

In short: RENTALL does not hold users' money. Rental payments are made directly between the renter and the owner outside the app. We do not sell your personal data.

1. Data we collect

DataPurpose
Email addressSigning in to your account (OTP code) and account communication.
Name and phone numberYour identity in the app and coordinating the handover of items. Your phone number is shown to the other party only after a rental or offer is approved.
Profile photo (optional)Shown on your profile and on owner/offer-maker cards.
Location (GPS)Showing how far items are from you, saving the location of items you list, and setting the meeting point. Location is requested only while the app is in use and you can decline it.
Item photos, damage evidence photosDisplaying listings and supporting problem reports.
Level 2 Verification: ID number (NIK), ID card photo, selfie photoVerifying identity for transactions worth Rp500,000 and above and for making offers in the Needs feature. Photos can only be taken with the camera.
Chat messages, reviews, ratings, accounts you followCommunication between users and building reputation.
Transaction data: bookings, schedules, Needs requests, offers, problem reportsRunning the service, keeping item availability accurate, and resolving disputes.
Payment info you type as an owner (for example bank account number/QRIS)Shown to the renter after the request is approved so they know how to pay you directly. We do not process that payment.

We do not collect contact data from your device, we do not use third-party ads, and we do not track your activity in other apps.

2. Who can see your data

  • Public in the app: name, profile photo, rating, reviews, verified status, the listings/packages you rent out, and the Needs requests you create.
  • The other party in a transaction: phone number, meeting point, handover time, and payment info, only after approval.
  • Not shared with other users: email, ID number, ID card and selfie photos, and your precise GPS location (except the meeting point you set yourself).

3. Third parties that help us

  • Supabase – database, authentication, and file storage.
  • Brevo – sending OTP code emails.
  • OCR.space – reading the text on ID card photos to match the ID number during verification.
  • Expo – infrastructure for building and updating the app.
  • Google Play – app distribution, and in the future payment for RENTALL paid features through Google Play Billing.
  • Google Maps (link) – the "Open in Google Maps" button only opens a map app/site outside RENTALL.

We only share the data needed for these services to work, and we may disclose data if required by law or an official request from the authorities.

4. Automated decisions about your account

To keep things safe without manual intervention, the system records violation points automatically (for example admitting damage, or not responding to a report within the time limit). An account that reaches three points within 90 days is temporarily restricted. The full rules are in the Terms & Conditions. You can contact us if you believe there is a mistake.

5. Storage and security

  • Data is stored with infrastructure providers that apply access protection; each user can only read the data they are entitled to.
  • ID card and selfie photos are stored in private (non-public) storage and are never shown again in the app.
  • No system is 100% secure. Never share your OTP code with anyone.

6. How long data is kept

  • While your account is active, data is kept so the service can run.
  • When an account is deleted: name, phone number, email, ID number, ID card/selfie photos, profile photo, listing photos, chat messages, follow lists, notifications, and Needs requests and offers are deleted. Listings are deactivated.
  • Rental history and problem reports are kept in anonymous form (the name becomes "Deleted User") for as long as needed to resolve disputes and protect other users. Reviews you gave remain without your identity.

7. Your rights

  • View and update your name, phone number, and profile photo directly in the app.
  • Delete your account: Profile → Delete Account, or through the Delete Account page.
  • Withdraw location, camera, or gallery permission at any time through your device settings.
  • Request a copy of your data, a correction, or object to processing: contact anthen666@gmail.com.

8. Children

RENTALL is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under that age. If you find any, contact us so the account can be deleted.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated. Important changes will be announced in the app, and the "Last updated" date above will be updated.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy: anthen666@gmail.com.
