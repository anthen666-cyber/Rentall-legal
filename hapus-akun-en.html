Delete Your RENTALL Account

Last updated: November 4, 2026 · Bahasa Indonesia

Option 1: directly in the app (fastest)

  1. Open the RENTALL app and sign in to your account.
  2. Go to the Profile tab → Delete Account.
  3. Read the information shown, type HAPUS in the confirmation field (in the English version of the app, the screen asks you to type DELETE), then press Delete My Account.

Option 2: by email (if you cannot open the app)

Send an email to anthen666@gmail.com with the subject Hapus Akun RENTALL (RENTALL account deletion) from the email address registered to your account. Write your account name and your deletion request. We process requests within 7 business days at most and will update you by email at the same address.

Requirements for deletion

  • No rentals still in progress or pending (as a renter or as an owner).
  • No jobs in the Needs feature still in progress.

Data that is deleted

  • Name, phone number, email address, and profile photo.
  • ID number (NIK), ID card photo, and verification selfie.
  • Photos and data of the items you list (listings are deactivated).
  • All chat messages, follow lists, and notifications.
  • Requests and offers in the Needs feature.
  • Meeting points and payment info on bookings, as well as links to report evidence photos.

Data that is kept (anonymous)

Rental history and problem reports are kept without identity (the name becomes "Deleted User") for as long as needed to resolve disputes and protect other users. Reviews you gave remain without your name.

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
